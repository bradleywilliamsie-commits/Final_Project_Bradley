About School:

It is a Catholic school and its name is from Saint Laurensius from Brindisi, a historical Catholic figure.



School Motto: Educatio Puerilis Renovatio Mundi Est

This motto means: Child Education Is the Renewal of the World. This motto signifies the vision and mission of our school, which is to educate thee children to be our future.
